'@avasapp/agent-bridge': patch
---

A session no longer runs a call twice when its reply is lost (the app reloaded or the connection dropped mid-call). Read-only tools such as `screen.snapshot`, `screen.waitFor` and `store.get` are repeated on the new connection (a `screen.waitFor` repeat gets only the time left, and a `{gone: true}` wait is not repeated after a reload); any other tool, including `bridge.restore` and app tools that only end in `.log`, fails with `the app reloaded during <tool>; it may or may not have run` (`the app disconnected` or `the connection dropped` when no reload is known), with the reload warning attached. That message also comes when the reconnect itself fails, with the reason added. The session's 5 s health check no longer takes the app's logged errors, so they reach the next real call tagged with the tool they followed, not `bridge.ping`. A `timeoutMs` in the options of `screen.waitFor` or `webview.waitFor` (`screen.waitFor '["Done", {"timeoutMs": 15000}]'`) longer than the call timeout now stretches it (2 s past it, 7 s for `webview.waitFor`, at most 10 minutes), in the CLI, sessions and `connect()`, so the app's own timeout error arrives first. Other tools' `timeoutMs` arguments are left alone.
