New `screen.setValue(target, value)` sets a Switch, Slider, Picker or SegmentedControl, and returns the value the app shows once it has rendered, with a warning when the app did not take it (not for a Slider, whose `value` is often only a start value). A Switch takes true or false. A Slider takes a number within `minimumValue` and `maximumValue` (0 and 1 when unset) and on its `step`, and gets `onSlidingStart`, `onValueChange` and `onSlidingComplete` in that order; a Slider with only `onSlidingComplete` works. A Picker takes one of its `Picker.Item` values (read from its children, as the native picker does) and gets the item index too. A SegmentedControl takes one of its `values` through `onChange` and `onValueChange`. A wrong type or value is an error that lists the options. `screen.press` on a Switch (found by its native switch host or `role="switch"`, so one whose `value` has not loaded yet counts) toggles it with `onValueChange` instead of pressing the row around it (the reply says `via: "onValueChange"`). `screen.press(target, {long:true})` long presses, calling `onPressIn`, `onLongPress` and `onPressOut`, and errors when there is no `onLongPress` on the element or before the first element that handles presses. `{scroll:true}` on `screen.press`, `screen.fill` and `screen.setValue` now pages the largest vertical `FlatList`, `SectionList` or `FlashList` for a row it has not rendered yet, outward from where it is, waiting for rows to render after each page (up to 40 pages or 3 seconds). When nothing matches it scrolls the list back to where it was, and the error says how far it got and which lists it left alone. Paging can trigger `onEndReached` and `onViewableItemsChanged`. `{scroll:true}` with `{at:[x,y]}` is refused. `screen.waitFor` does not page lists: wait for the list, then use `press`, `fill` or `setValue` with `{scroll:true}`. Pressing something inside a react-native-gesture-handler tap or long press `GestureDetector` fails with a message saying its callbacks run on the UI thread, and no longer falls through to a press handler further up.
